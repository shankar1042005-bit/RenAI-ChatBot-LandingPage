How is RenAI different from a normal chatbot?

A general-purpose chatbot relies primarily on its training data. RenAI’s retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) approach retrieves relevant information from company-controlled knowledge before generating an answer. That makes the response specific to your business, rather than just plausible.

What knowledge can I use with RenAI?

The experience is designed around business documents, product guides, help articles, website content, and other customer-facing knowledge. In this walkthrough, you can explore sample team, billing, and integration guides. Actual ingestion and file-format support are part of the production integration, not this static demo.

Can RenAI be used in web and mobile applications?

Yes—the architecture separates the knowledge and answer-generation layer from the customer interface. That allows a website widget, an in-app chat, or a mobile interface to use the same underlying knowledge. Explore both interface concepts in the developer section above.

What happens when the knowledge doesn’t have an answer?

Trustworthy support should acknowledge missing information instead of inventing an answer. RenAI’s intended approach is to stay within available context and guide the customer toward human help when needed. You can try an out-of-scope question in this walkthrough to see that behavior illustrated.

Is the interactive demo connected to a live AI model?

No. This is a self-contained product walkthrough with prewritten answers and sample knowledge. It illustrates retrieval, source citations, and responsible fallback behavior without sending your questions to an external service. A production RenAI integration would require a backend, knowledge ingestion, and a connected AI model.
